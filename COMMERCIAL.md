# Commercial use of ParVu

ParVu is free for noncommercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md). Using it for commercial purposes needs a paid commercial license.

## Free: no license needed

- personal use: your own data, study, hobby projects, research and experiments with no commercial application;
- charities, educational institutions, public research organisations, public safety or health organisations, environmental protection organisations and government institutions, whatever the funding.

The license text defines these in "Personal Uses" and "Noncommercial Organizations".

## Paid: commercial license

You need a commercial license to use ParVu:
- at work in a company, including internal analysis, reporting and data checks;
- as a freelancer or consultant for paid work;
- in a product or service you sell;
- if you redistribute it, or a modified version, commercially.

A commercial license covers the version you license and the updates within its term. It replaces the noncommercial terms for that use.

## How to get one

Write to the author through [Telegram](https://t.me/aziz_nadirov) or [open an issue](https://github.com/AzizNadirov/ParVu/issues) titled "Commercial license" with:
- the company name;
- the number of users;
- how ParVu will be used.

You'll get the terms and a price.

## Earlier versions

Versions up to and including 1.0.0-alpha.8 were published with "MIT" in their package metadata. This change applies to later versions.

## Third-party components

ParVu includes open-source libraries under their own licenses, mostly MIT and Apache-2.0, a few MPL-2.0. Those licenses apply to those components and are not changed by ParVu's license.
